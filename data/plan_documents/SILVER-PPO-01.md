# §1 Definitions

- *Emergency medical condition:* a condition with acute symptoms severe enough that a prudent layperson would expect serious harm without immediate care.
- *Cosmetic:* a service performed mainly to change or improve appearance rather than to restore or improve bodily function.
- *Experimental or investigational:* a service not generally accepted as safe and effective for the condition.

# §2 Eligibility

Coverage applies to services received while the member's policy is active, as shown in the plan's enrollment records.

# §3.1 In-network services

Services from in-network providers are covered at the in-network level.

# §3.2 Out-of-network services

Services from out-of-network providers are covered at the out-of-network level.

# §3.3 Emergency services

Emergency services are covered at the in-network level from any provider.

# §4.1 Office visits

Office visits with a primary care provider or specialist.

# §4.2 Emergency department

Emergency department services for an emergency medical condition.

# §4.3 Diagnostic tests and imaging

Diagnostic tests and imaging, including laboratory tests, electrocardiograms and X-rays. Advanced imaging (MRI, CT, PET) requires prior authorization.

# §4.4 Rehabilitation therapy

Rehabilitation therapy (physical, occupational and speech therapy).

# §4.5 Mental health

Mental health and substance use disorder services, covered on the same terms as medical services.

# §4.6 Bariatric surgery

Bariatric surgery, with prior authorization.

# §4.7 Reconstructive surgery

Reconstructive surgery, including all stages of breast reconstruction after a mastectomy and surgery to correct a congenital anomaly or an accidental injury.

# §4.8 Acupuncture

Acupuncture.

# §5.1 Experimental services

Experimental or investigational services are excluded.

# §5.2 Cosmetic services

This exclusion does not apply to: (a) reconstructive services under §4.7; (b) surgery to correct a functional impairment, such as upper-eyelid surgery when excess skin obstructs the visual field; (c) repair of an accidental injury.

# §6 Prior authorization

Services marked as requiring prior authorization must be approved before they are provided. A claim for such a service without an approval on file is pended for the authorization, not denied, unless the service was an emergency.
