# MP-001.1 Conservative therapy

At least 6 weeks of documented conservative treatment (physical therapy, medication, or activity modification) without adequate improvement.

# MP-001.2 Red flags

Any of: progressive neurological deficit, history of cancer, suspected infection, significant trauma, or cauda equina symptoms.
