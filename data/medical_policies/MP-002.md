# MP-002.1 BMI

BMI ≥ 40, or BMI ≥ 35 with a serious obesity-related condition (type 2 diabetes, hypertension, sleep apnea).

# MP-002.2 Supervised weight loss

A documented, physician-supervised weight-loss attempt.

# MP-002.3 Psychological evaluation

A pre-operative psychological evaluation clearing the member for surgery.
